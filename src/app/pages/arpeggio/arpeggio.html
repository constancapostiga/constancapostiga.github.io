ARPEGGIO MUSIC STORE (2025)

Branding Social Media Stationery Merch

For this project, I developed the visual identity for Arpeggio, a fictional music store located in Porto. I wanted to use this project as an opportunity to explore a more playful, expressive and unconventional visual language, moving away from some of the more restrained work I had created previously.

Arpeggio is imagined as a space for teenagers and young adults who share a passion for music, instruments and records — a place where discovering music feels as exciting as experiencing it. More than simply a store, the concept behind Arpeggio is a place where you can live the music: a space that encourages people to explore, discover new sounds and connect with music in a more personal and creative way.

The name Arpeggio comes from the musical technique. This concept became an important part of the visual identity, particularly in the logo, whose movement is inspired by the rhythm and progression of an arpeggio. I wanted the identity to communicate a sense of movement, rhythm and energy, while still being simple enough to remain recognisable and versatile across different applications.

To bring the identity to life, I created a range of playful stationery and merchandise, including tote bags and other branded materials. These applications were designed to make the store feel contemporary, approachable and culturally relevant to its target audience. The goal was for the branding to work not only inside the store, but also as something people would want to interact with and take with them. They were designed to function almost as a piece of advertising — something eye-catching and desirable that could spark curiosity about the brand simply by being seen on the street. Through these different touchpoints, I wanted Arpeggio to feel like a brand that people would remember, want to visit for the first time, and ultimately want to return to.

Overall, the project allowed me to experiment with a more playful and youthful approach to branding, while exploring how a visual identity can translate the feeling and energy of music into a physical space and a wider brand experience.

BAR - PRAIA DO TURISMO (2026)

Tools used for this project:

and AI for mockups

For this project, I developed an alternative visual identity for Praia do Turismo, a seasonal beach bar located in Vila do Conde, Portugal. This was my first opportunity to work with a real client, allowing me to experience a design process that went beyond the academic context and consider how a brand identity could respond to the needs of an existing business.

Praia do Turismo is a space defined by its privileged seaside location, welcoming atmosphere and diverse clientele. It is a place where people can enjoy a drink with friends, relax while watching the sunset, stop for a quick coffee before returning to work, or simply spend time by the beach. Its appeal lies in its versatility: it can be a lively meeting point for social gatherings or a peaceful place to unwind, welcoming people of different ages and backgrounds.

The challenge was to create an identity that reflected this balance between relaxation and sophistication, informality and elegance. I wanted the new visual direction to preserve the welcoming character of the bar while giving it a more contemporary, distinctive and consistent presence. Another important consideration was its seasonal nature and the need for a stronger visual communication strategy, particularly across social media, to help maintain the brand's presence beyond the summer months.

The project began with research into the bar, its surroundings and its existing audience, followed by an analysis of competitors and relevant visual references. This process helped me identify opportunities for differentiation and establish the values that would guide the identity: lightness, clarity, authenticity, simplicity, comfort and elegance.
Based on these foundations, I explored different visual directions and developed a logo that combines a more refined typographic approach with a relaxed sun symbol. This contrast reflects the duality of the space, bringing together a sense of sophistication and the easy-going atmosphere associated with a beach bar. The colour palette, centred around navy blue and yellow, was inspired by the sea and the sun, while also helping to communicate both professionalism and energy.

Once the identity was established, I developed a range of applications designed to create a coherent brand experience across both physical and digital touchpoints. A particular focus of the project was ensuring that the identity worked in a real-world context. For example, the menus were designed with the beach environment in mind, considering factors such as outdoor use, sunlight and ease of handling. The digital menu and social media templates were developed to support the bar's communication and make it easier to share information about opening times, events and seasonal activities.

This project was an important step in my development as a designer, as it allowed me to experience the challenges of working with a real client while strengthening my skills in branding, visual communication, research and design strategy. It also taught me the importance of testing ideas, adapting solutions and considering how a visual identity functions across different contexts.

Ultimately, the project allowed me to explore how design can help a familiar space evolve without losing its character, creating an identity that feels both recognisable and renewed, welcoming and distinctive.

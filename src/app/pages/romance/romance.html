ROMANCE ICE CREAM (2025)

For this university project, I was challenged to create a fictional campaign and packaging concept for a special edition of an existing food product. I chose Continente’s ice cream cups and developed Romance, a limited-edition line created around Valentine’s Day and, more broadly, the idea of celebrating love and romantic gestures.

The concept was based on transforming an everyday product into something that feels more special and giftable. Rather than limiting the campaign to Valentine’s Day, I wanted Romance to celebrate the small moments shared between people whether it is a date, a spontaneous gesture or simply enjoying something sweet together.

For the special edition, I developed three flavours: Strawberry and Chocolate, Blueberry Cheesecake and Red Velvet Cake. Each flavour was given its own colour and visual identity while maintaining a strong connection to the overall Romance collection.

To bring the three products together, I created a geometric pattern inspired by the idea of connection and repetition. The pattern became a key element of the packaging and campaign, creating a visual system that allowed the three different flavours to feel like part of the same product family. Each container uses a different colour to distinguish the flavour, while the shared graphic elements maintain consistency across the range.

The goal was to create packaging that felt playful, contemporary and visually appealing, while still fitting naturally within the Continente brand. By keeping the existing brand recognition and combining it with a new visual language, the project explores how packaging can transform a familiar product into a limited-edition experience.

The final outcome was a cohesive three-product collection where each flavour can stand on its own, while the three cups become more visually interesting when presented together. The campaign and packaging were designed to communicate the idea that love can be found in the simple things, even in sharing an ice cream.

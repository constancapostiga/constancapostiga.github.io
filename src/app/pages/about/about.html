I'm a 21-year-old Graphic Designer based in Porto, Portugal. I graduated UPCA with a degree in Graphic Design in June 2026. I'm passionate about visual communication, branding and creative storytelling, and I enjoy transforming ideas and concepts into visual identities that are not only aesthetically engaging, but also meaningful, purposeful and memorable. For me, design is about more than creating something that looks good. It's about understanding the idea behind a project, the people it is intended for and the message it needs to communicate. I enjoy exploring how typography, colour, composition, imagery and visual systems can work together to create a clear and engaging experience. I'm naturally curious and always looking for new ways to explore design and expand my creative thinking. I enjoy experimenting with different styles, techniques and approaches, and I'm not afraid to step outside of my comfort zone when a project calls for something different. I'm particularly interested in branding, editorial design, UX/UI, packaging and social media, where I can combine creativity with strategy and attention to detail. I also have a strong interest in illustration, which I enjoy incorporating into projects whenever it feels like the right way to communicate an idea. Throughout my studies and projects, I've also learned how important collaboration and teamwork are within the creative process. I enjoy working with other people, sharing ideas and seeing how different perspectives can transform and improve an initial concept. I'm comfortable both contributing my own ideas and listening to feedback, and I see constructive criticism as an important part of developing better work. I believe that the best creative outcomes often come from being open to other perspectives and being willing to adapt an idea rather than becoming attached to the first solution. I'm always looking for new challenges, opportunities to learn and people to collaborate with. I want to continue evolving as a designer, discovering new areas of the creative industry and working on projects that allow me to turn ideas into something people can see, feel and remember. Ultimately, I'm still discovering what kind of designer I want to become and that's one of the things I enjoy most about design. There is always something new to learn, experiment with and create.
